# Gladdy

## Description
Gladdy helps you track enemy players in arenas efficiently, so you can anticipate their actions and make smarter plays in PvP.

Provides:
- Arena enemy frames showing health, spec, and important cooldowns
- Alerts for trinkets, crowd control, and defensive abilities
- Easy-to-read information for quick reaction in PvP
- Optional customization of frames and icons

## Setup
1. Download the addon: `Gladdy (Classic)`
2. Log in to the game:
    - Type __/gladdy ui__
    - Go to `Export Import` > Click on `Import`
    - Copy and paste the following text - [gladdy profile](gladdy_profile.txt)
    - Press `Import`
